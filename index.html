HEURISTIC APPROACH

⚡ Greedy Algorithm

Assigns each appointment to the best currently available slot according to the scheduling rules. It is fast, but a locally best choice may not produce the global best schedule.

  • Sorts requests by priority and requested date
  • Chooses the earliest available valid slot
  • Low computational overhead
EVOLUTIONARY APPROACH

🧬 Genetic Algorithm

Searches for a better overall assignment using candidate schedules that evolve through selection, crossover and mutation.

  • Creates a population of candidate schedules
  • Uses fitness-based selection and mutation
  • Balances priority, waiting and slot usage
